The inspiration behind

Even though I’m a Red Hot Chili Peppers fan, I came across their latest album, Return of the Dream Canteen, four years late — and still fell completely in love with it, the music and the art around it. The album came with a series of psychedelic visualizers: fluid, morphing shapes that moved along with the sound. I wanted to recreate that effect myself, so what you’re seeing on this site is a hand-crafted WebGL shader, built from scratch to translate that same movement into real-time code.
