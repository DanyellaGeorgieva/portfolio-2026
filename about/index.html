I’m Daniela Paneva —
a front-end developer based in Sofia, Bulgaria

I build things that move, react, and feel alive. Most of my work centers on creative coding and interaction, where I spend real time on animation curves, transitions, and micro-interactions — tuning them to feel natural instead of distracting.

The inspiration behind this site

Even though I'm a Red Hot Chili Peppers fan, I came across their latest album, Return of the Dream Canteen, four years late — and still fell completely in love with it, the music and the art around it. The album came with a series of psychedelic, liquid visualizers: fluid, morphing shapes that moved along with the sound. I wanted to recreate that effect myself, so what you're seeing on this site is a hand-crafted WebGL shader, built from scratch to translate that same movement into real-time code.
