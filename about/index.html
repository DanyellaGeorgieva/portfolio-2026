I’m Daniela Paneva — a front-end developer based in Sofia, Bulgaria.

I build things that move, react, and feel alive. Most of my work centers on creative coding and interaction, where I spend real time on animation curves, transitions, and micro-interactions — tuning them to feel natural instead of distracting.

The inspiration behind this site

Even though I'm a Red Hot Chili Peppers fan, I came across their latest album, Return of the Dream Canteen, four years late — and still fell completely in love with it, the music and the art around it. The album came with a series of psychedelic, liquid visualizers — fluid, morphing shapes that moved along with the sound. I wanted to recreate that same effect myself, translated into real-time WebGL shader code.

Tools & Setup

Vanilla JavaScript / WebGL / Three.js / CSS architecture / Creative layout strategy
