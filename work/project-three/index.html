Agency site · 2025

NEXT-DC AGENCY SITE

Next DC is an independent agency working out of Sofia, London and Amsterdam, with PUMA, Twitter, BMW, Carlsberg, WWF and Jack Daniel’s on the client list. This is their own site — which meant the brief was the hardest kind: it had to demonstrate the standard the studio sells, with no client to blame for compromises.

Role
Lead front-end developer. Every page except Team, which a colleague completed after I went on leave.
Concept & identity
Next DC

A work grid made of video

The case study grid loads video per tile, mixing .webp and .mp4. That’s the classic agency-site trap: the thing that sells the studio is also the thing that destroys load performance, and every agency site is judged on speed by exactly the people who would hire the agency.

Theming across a whole site

There’s a theme switcher. Doing that properly across an entire site — every surface, every state, media that was authored for one background appearing on another — is much harder than it looks, and it’s the same instinct that later became the palette-per-section system on my own portfolio.

The reveal system

Every headline exists twice in the DOM — one copy clipped, one visible — driving a masked line-by-line reveal on load and on scroll. Applied consistently across every page, in a way that had to survive arbitrary CMS copy at any length, in two scripts.

The rest of the build

  • A custom CMS rather than a site builder, so the templates had to hold up against content nobody had written yet.
  • A percentage loader.

Outcome

I handed the project over one section short of finished and went on maternity leave. A colleague completed the Team page.
