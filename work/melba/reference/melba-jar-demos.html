Collider and skin

Every object leads a double life. Matter.js only simulates the collider: a deliberately simple polygon of three to twelve points, hidden in the page as an SVG. Visitors only ever see the skin, the illustration laid on top. The one concave outline, the leaf, gets cut into convex pieces first, because that is all the engine can handle.

Skins: what visitors saw. Colliders: what the physics engine simulates. The outlines are the real ones from the live site; the shaded pieces are the leaf's convex parts, and the dashed shapes are the invisible walls hiding behind the drawn glass. Hover or drag across the jar to nudge the contents.

A ring of dots pretending to be jelly

The blob is a ring of invisible dots. Each dot is rigidly linked to its neighbour, springs loosely to the dot two places along, and is tethered to where it started. A smooth curve drawn through the dots every frame does the rest.

Tether pulls each dot back home. Shape spring stops the ring folding in on itself. Original is the Review jar's tuning: 16 dots, tether 0.009, shape spring 0.04. Poke the blob, or shake the jar.
